# PepMod 1.0.6 (Build 22)

## App Store — What’s New

Build protocols around the vial and schedule you actually use.

- Track pre-blended vials as one dose while preserving every constituent.
- Save custom compounds once and reuse them across protocols.
- Create week-to-week phases with their own weekdays, times, amounts, and notes.
- Convert user-entered IU vial amounts to U-100 syringe markings without dose recommendations.
- See localized App Store pricing and correct previously logged doses.

PepMod remains an educational tracking and unit-conversion tool. It does not recommend doses or mixing plans.

## App Review Notes

Version 1.0.6 expands tracking and arithmetic workflows without providing medical advice:

- **Pre-blended vial:** In Protocol, create a protocol and choose **Pre-blended vial**. Enter the constituent amounts shown on the vial label, diluent volume, and intended U-100 draw. PepMod schedules and logs one event and shows the arithmetic amount of each constituent per draw.
- **Saved compounds:** Open Library or Profile, choose **My compounds**, and tap **Add compound**. Saved entries can be reused in protocol creation. Archive/restore is reversible and does not rewrite historical dose records.
- **Phased schedule:** While creating or editing a protocol, enable phases. Each week range can have its own selected weekdays, multiple times, amount, unit, syringe units, and notes.
- **IU conversion:** Open the conversion workspace and select IU. Both vial and intended amount remain in IU; PepMod only calculates the corresponding U-100 syringe marking and does not convert IU to mass units or recommend an amount.
